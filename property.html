What does k.k. mean?

K.k. stands for kosten koper. The buyer pays the costs of the purchase on top of the price, such as transfer tax and the notary fee for the deed of transfer.

What is the difference between freehold and leasehold?

With freehold you own the home and the ground it stands on. With leasehold you use ground owned by someone else, often the municipality, under set terms and sometimes for a yearly fee. Ask your agent which applies and what the terms are.

What is a VvE?

A VvE is the owners’ association of a shared building. Owners pay into it and decide together about maintenance. Ask for the monthly fee, the reserve fund and the maintenance plan.
